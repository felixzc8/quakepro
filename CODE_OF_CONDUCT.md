# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful. Welcome different experience and viewpoints. Give useful, specific
feedback. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share private information, or
disrupt discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban people whose behavior
breaks these standards.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's reporting tools, or open an issue with
only details safe to share publicly. Maintainers will handle reports fairly and
keep details limited to people who need them.
